# Tesla noise evidence catalogue

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

This catalogue supports the `/listen/` sound-triage taxonomy. It does **not** copy, download, train on, or redistribute third-party audio. Links are citations and examples only.

## Evidence labels

- **Official procedure** — public Tesla service information supporting a physical inspection step.
- **Repair-confirmed example** — source reports a repair and post-repair resolution or comparison.
- **Owner-claimed example** — creator reports a cause or fix, but the outcome is not independently verified.
- **Demonstration only** — useful sound example without a sufficiently documented causal repair.

A YouTube example is evidence that a symptom/cause pairing has occurred on an individual vehicle, not evidence that the same sound always has that cause.

## Inclusion policy for future data

A case may inform the public taxonomy when it has:

1. an identifiable Tesla model or platform;
2. a reproducible operating condition;
3. a stated physical cause;
4. a repair outcome or clearly labeled weaker evidence grade;
5. no request to copy copyrighted audio.

Training/validation data requires separate consent and licensing. Prefer owner-submitted recordings with mechanic-confirmed outcomes, phone placement, vehicle configuration, and explicit permission for the intended use.
